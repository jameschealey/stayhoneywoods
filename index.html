The house

Welcome to Honeywoods, a newly listed manor that completed renovations in November 2026: right in time for Thanksgiving & the holidays!

Designed by an award-winning hotelier, Honeywoods is a Hudson Valley retreat that invites you to gather, cook, read, create, and explore. Enjoy the home’s sweeping views, library, private study, arts & crafts station, games parlour, fireplace, chef’s kitchen, fantasy-themed bedrooms, hobby supplies/kits, and…who knows what else you may discover?

The whole house is yours for the stay.

The area

Stanfordville is a small hamlet in Dutchess County, in the Hudson Valley.

Wing's Castle is about 10 miles away and Millbrook Vineyards about 11. The Old Rhinebeck Aerodrome and the Omega Institute are also close. The town park in Pine Plains is about 5 miles from the house.

Good to know

Arrival

Check-in is from 2:00 PM and checkout is by 11:00 AM. Please tell us your arrival time in advance.

What's included

A full kitchen with a coffee machine, Wi-Fi, air conditioning, a washing machine, a TV, a work desk and free parking at the house. Outside there is a deck and a garden.

House rules

No smoking, no pets, and no parties or events. Children of all ages are welcome. Cribs and extra beds are not available.

Getting here

Stewart International Airport is 47 miles away and Albany International Airport is 61 miles.
